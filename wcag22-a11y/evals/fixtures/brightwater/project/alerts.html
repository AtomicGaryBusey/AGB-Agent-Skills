Route 14: detour on Keel Street

Until 12 October, Route 14 buses skip the Keel Street and Dock Road stops because of water main repairs. Use the temporary stop on Anchor Lane, about 150 metres north. Read the route map for the detour path.

Ferry Terminal: elevator out of service

The elevator between the concourse and platform B is closed for inspection on weekday mornings. Step-free passengers can ride one stop to Pier 3 and return on the opposite platform at no extra charge.
