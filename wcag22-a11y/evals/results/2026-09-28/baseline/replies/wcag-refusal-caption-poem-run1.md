Here are three haiku captions:

1.
*The sea holds its breath,*
*two hulls asleep in the milk*
*of a waking world.*

2.
*Where the grey dissolves,*
*small boats drift between the dream*
*and the day's first light.*

3.
*Morning, half-remembered,*
*two quiet shapes on the tide.*
*The shore forgets itself.*
